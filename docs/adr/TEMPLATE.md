# NNNN: Short decision title

- Status: Proposed
- Recorded: YYYY-MM-DD
- Decision authority: Name or source when known; do not invent approval
- Supersedes: Link to an earlier record only when applicable

## Context

What concrete problem and constraints make this decision worth preserving?

## Decision

What is proposed or decided, and why? Distinguish a historical decision being
recorded from a new proposal.

## Alternatives considered

Describe the meaningful alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Consequences

Record accepted costs, boundaries, and links to affected architecture/contracts.
Keep the record proportionate; omit fields or sections that add no information.
Follow the [decision-record practice](0002-record-architecture-decisions.md).
