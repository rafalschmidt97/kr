# Wedding and story language

Terms used by the couple, guests, and repository authors. Implementation paths
belong in the [architecture map](docs/architecture.md).

## Language

**Para Młoda**: Klaudia and Rafał as the wedding couple.

**Ceremonia ślubna**: The church ceremony, distinct from the reception at Villa
Presto. _Avoid_: using "wesele" when referring only to the church service.

**Wesele**: The wedding celebration and reception, including the meal and
dancing. The website's overall invitation can refer to the wedding day broadly.

**Poprawiny**: The separate, less formal Sunday gathering for its invited group.
_Avoid_: treating it as an invitation automatically extended to every guest.

**Plan dnia**: The guest-facing sequence of wedding-day events.
_Avoid_: "harmonogram Pary Młodej" for this public overview.

**Harmonogram Pary Młodej**: The couple's detailed working schedule, including
preparations, responsibilities, and unsettled arrangements.

**Grupa gości**: Guests sharing a logistics arrangement, such as staying at the
same hotel. It is an audience for information, not a named guest list.

**Potwierdzenie obecności (RSVP)**: A guest's direct confirmation to the couple
about attendance. _Avoid_: calling a wedding prediction an RSVP.

**Do potwierdzenia**: A provisional detail that still needs confirmation.
**Do ustalenia**: An arrangement for which a decision is still missing.
_Avoid_: presenting either as a finalized booking or time.

**Nasza historia**: The couple's relationship narrative, organized into chapters.
**Rozdział**: One part of that narrative, not an individual dated milestone.

**Archiwalna oś czasu**: The earlier dated mapping of memories and photographs,
retained as an archive. _Avoid_: describing it as the current story presentation.

**Weselne przewidywania**: A playful prediction about what might happen at the
wedding. _Avoid_: "zgłoszenie", "głosowanie gości", or "wyniki" when describing
the current individual preview.
