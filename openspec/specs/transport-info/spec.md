# transport-info Specification

## Purpose

Define shared church-to-reception and return transport guidance within the
current plan and FAQ, including provisional times.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Shared transport information

The plan and FAQ SHALL describe shared transport between the church and Villa Presto and the return after the reception. Group-specific travel guidance SHALL remain on the relevant direct-link group pages.

#### Scenario: Shared buses shown

- **WHEN** a visitor reads the plan and FAQ
- **THEN** it states there is a bus from the church to Villa Presto after the ceremony
- **AND** it states there are return buses from Villa Presto

#### Scenario: Times may be pending

- **WHEN** exact bus times are not yet finalized
- **THEN** provisional times in the plan are clearly marked as awaiting confirmation
